RFM analysis on 1.07M+ transactions from 5,878 customers, segmented into 5 groups via K-Means clustering. Validated against Gaussian Mixture and DBSCAN models with stability testing across resampled subsets, reaching a mean Adjusted Rand Index of 0.98.

Premier League Match Predictor

Python · scikit-learn · Streamlit

Multi-output linear regression predicting Premier League scorelines from 10 seasons of match and betting-odds data (R² = 0.42, MAE = 0.74 goals). Documents its own limitations — it can't capture rare high-scoring outliers — and proposes Poisson regression as the fix.
